Communications & Radioamateurs

Help us collect telemetry data and become part of the mission.

Track Nyxsat

The Nyxsat team actively encourages the global amateur radio community to listen out for the satellite's telemetry beacon. Your reports are incredibly valuable to us, especially during the early orbit phase.

Frequencies & Modulation

  • Downlink Frequency: 437.425 MHz (UHF)
  • Beacon: 9600 bps GMSK
  • Protocol: AX.25 UI frames
  • Callsign: IR0AB

QSL Cards

We will happily send a special mission QSL card to any radio amateur who submits valid telemetry packets. Instructions for decoding the telemetry and submitting your data will be provided on this page shortly before launch.
